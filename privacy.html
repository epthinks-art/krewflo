PRIVACY

Your information, in plain language

Your customer photos and transcripts stay in your own accounts. Nothing is sent to a customer without your approval.

What the demo form collects

When you request a demo, the form collects your name, company, email address, phone number (optional), the industry you pick, and any message you include. The form is delivered by email through a service called FormSubmit.

How we use it

We use these details only to contact you about a demo and to answer your questions. We don’t add you to a mailing list without asking.

We don’t sell your data

We never sell or rent your information to anyone.

Your clients’ data

When you use Krewflo, your inspection photos, transcripts, and customer details stay in your own accounts. Krewflo drafts reports and emails for you to review, and nothing goes to a customer unless you send it.

Questions or removal requests

Email ethan@krewflo.com or call (805) 242-9502, and we’ll answer or delete your information.
